About the clinic
Bluehill Kids Clinic is a children's clinic in Northfield.
We have two doctors and one front desk.
We opened in 2019 and see about 40 children a day.
This summary follows the order of the topics in the meeting.

Our doctors
Dr. Nisha Rao, Paediatrics.
Dr. Thomas Kurian, Child development.
Parents should see each doctor's specialty before they choose a slot.

Our patients
Most visits are check-ups, vaccines and visits for fever or a cough.
Visits for fever are the most common in the rainy season.
Parents usually bring the child's health card to the visit.

Booking rules
We book children in 20-minute slots.
Parents can book up to 7 days ahead.
Children up to 12 years can be booked.
Parents use their booking code to look up a visit.

The booking pages
The site should feel friendly for parents.
Each doctor has their own calendar of free slots.

The home page
The home page shows our name, address, opening hours and phone number.
Please use the photos of the waiting room that Priya sends.
We would like a short line under the name: "A clinic for children in Northfield."

The admin page
The admin page holds the list of doctors, their weekly hours and the list of bookings.

What parents ask us most
Parents ask about our opening hours, how to reach us, and which doctor to see for their child.
We hope the home page and the doctor list answer these questions on the website.

Contact for the project
I am the contact for the clinic. If I am not available, please write to the clinic email address.
