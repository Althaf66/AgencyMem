Bluehill Kids Clinic: kickoff meeting notes
Wednesday 8 October, 11 am, at the clinic. Notes by Asha Nair, delivery lead.

Attendees
Dr. Nisha Rao, paediatrician and owner, Bluehill Kids Clinic.
Dr. Thomas Kurian, doctor, Bluehill Kids Clinic.
Priya, front desk, Bluehill Kids Clinic.
Asha Nair, delivery lead, and Vikram Shah, developer, from the agency.

Why the clinic wants a website
Parents call the clinic to book a visit for their child, and Priya writes each visit in a diary.
Dr. Rao says the phone rings most at 8 am, when parents call before work and school.
Parents who cannot get through come to the counter instead, and the waiting room fills up.
Dr. Rao wants parents to book online at any time of day, so the phone stays free for urgent calls.

What we showed
Asha showed the booking flow from an earlier project: pick a doctor, pick a slot, fill a form.
Vikram showed the admin page of the same project.
After the meeting, Dr. Rao showed the team the waiting room and the two consulting rooms.
Asha will set up a shared folder for the project files and send Dr. Rao the link.
Dr. Rao said that parents book on their phones, often while they wait outside the school.
Priya asked whether the site works on the front desk computer, which has an older browser.
Vikram will test the site on that browser before the first demo.

Discussion about the home page
Priya said that parents ask for directions on the phone almost every day.
Asha suggested a short line under the address on how to reach the clinic by bus.
Dr. Rao agreed and will send the text.

Questions from the meeting
Dr. Kurian asked how long the agency needs for the first version. Asha said two weeks.
Priya asked who to call with a question about the website. Asha will be the contact.

The clinic
The clinic is open Monday to Friday.
Opening hours are 10 am to 6 pm.
There is no lunch break; the two doctors take their lunch at different times.
Each visit slot is 15 minutes.
One slot is for one child with one doctor.

Pages we agreed on
1. A home page with the clinic name, address, opening hours and phone number.
2. A list of the doctors, with a filter by specialty.
3. A calendar of free slots for the chosen doctor.
4. A booking form for the chosen slot.
5. A page where a parent enters a booking code to see the booking or cancel it.
6. An admin page: doctors, weekly hours and the list of bookings.
The clinic is at 27 Bluehill Road, Northfield, and the phone number is 0491 570 158.

Calendar
Show enough slots so parents can choose.
A slot that is already booked does not appear as free.

Booking rules
Parents can book up to 30 days ahead.
The booking form asks for the parent's name, the child's name, the child's age and a phone number.
A child can have one booking per day.
Each booking gets a booking code of 6 characters, made of letters and digits.

Cancellation
Parents can cancel up to 1 hour before the visit.

Not part of this release
Patient accounts. Reminders by text message or email.

Tools at the clinic today
The front desk has one computer and a printer.
The diary stays in use until the website is live.
Next week's visits from the diary will be entered into the website before it goes live.
