About the clinic
This summary follows the order of the topics in the meeting.
Lotus Family Clinic is a family practice in Hillview with three doctors.
We see adults and children for general check-ups, child health and skin problems.

Our doctors
Dr. Anita Menon, General medicine.
Dr. Rahul Varma, Paediatrics.
Dr. Sameera Iqbal, Dermatology.
Patients should be able to see each doctor's specialty before they choose.

Booking rules
Appointments are booked in 30-minute slots.
Doctors see patients without a break from 9 am to 5 pm.
Patients can book up to 14 days ahead.
A phone number is required for every booking.
The patient's name is required as well.
The reason for the visit is optional and at most 200 characters.
Cancellation is not allowed on the day of the appointment.

The booking pages
The calendar should feel easy to use.
Each doctor has their own calendar of free slots.
When a slot is booked, no other patient can book it.
Patients use their booking code to look up a booking or to cancel it.

The admin page
Only the front desk uses the admin page.
Two staff members work at the front desk: Meena in the morning and Latha in the afternoon.
Both of them will use the admin page, so please show them how it works.

Look and feel
