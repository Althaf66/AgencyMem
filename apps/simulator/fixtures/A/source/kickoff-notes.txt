Lotus Family Clinic: kickoff meeting notes
Monday 6 October, 10 am, at the clinic. Notes by Asha Nair, delivery lead.

Attendees
Ravi, clinic manager, Lotus Family Clinic.
Dr. Anita Menon, doctor, Lotus Family Clinic.
Asha Nair, delivery lead, and Vikram Shah, developer, from the agency.

Why the clinic wants a website
Today patients call the front desk to book, and the front desk writes each booking in a diary.
Ravi says the front desk takes about 60 calls a day, and most of them are about bookings.
On busy mornings patients wait on hold, and the front desk cannot greet people at the counter.
Ravi wants patients to book online, so the phone line stays free for questions.

What we showed
Asha showed the booking flow from an earlier project: pick a doctor, pick a slot, fill a form.
Ravi and Dr. Menon agreed that this order of steps fits the clinic.
Vikram showed the admin page of the same project.
After the meeting, Ravi showed the team the front desk and the waiting room.
Asha will set up a shared folder for the project files and send Ravi the link.

Tools at the clinic today
The front desk has one computer with a web browser and a shared email address.
The diary stays in use until the website is live, then the front desk stops using it.
Ravi does not want the staff to install any new software on the front desk computer.

Clinic hours and slots
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 5 pm.
No appointments between 1 pm and 2 pm because of lunch.
Each appointment slot is 20 minutes long.
One slot is for one patient with one doctor.

Pages we agreed on
1. A home page with the clinic name, address, opening hours and phone number.
2. A list of the doctors, with a filter by specialty.
3. A calendar of free slots for the chosen doctor.
4. A booking form for the chosen slot.
5. A page where a patient enters a booking code to see the booking or cancel it.
6. An admin page for the front desk: doctors, weekly hours and the list of bookings.
The clinic address is 12 Lake View Road, Hillview, and the phone number is 0491 570 156.

Calendar
Show a few days of free slots.
A slot that is already booked does not appear as free.

Booking codes and limits
Each booking gets a booking code of 6 characters, made of letters and digits.
A patient can make one booking per phone number per day.
Ravi wants the limit because patients have booked two doctors on one day and then missed one.

Cancellation
Patients may cancel up to 2 hours before the appointment.
A patient cancels with the booking code on the lookup page.

Website
The website should load fast.
Ravi will send the clinic logo and two photos of the reception area.

Not part of this release
Online payment. Patients pay at the counter, as they do today.

Open points for the next meeting
Vikram will bring a first version of the calendar and the booking form.
Ravi will send a written summary of the clinic rules by Wednesday.
Ravi will check the doctors' weekly hours with each doctor.

Next steps
