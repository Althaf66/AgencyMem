Palm Grove Physio Centre: kickoff meeting notes
Tuesday 7 October, 2 pm, at the centre. Notes by Asha Nair, delivery lead.

Attendees
Sara, front desk lead, Palm Grove Physio Centre.
Meera Das, senior therapist, Palm Grove Physio Centre.
Asha Nair, delivery lead, and Vikram Shah, developer, from the agency.

Why the centre wants a website
Patients book therapy sessions by phone or at the counter, and Sara keeps the schedule in a spreadsheet.
Sara spends about two hours a day on booking calls and on moving sessions between therapists.
Patients who start a course of treatment call again after each session to book the next one.
Sara wants patients to book and cancel online, so the front desk can focus on patients in the centre.

What we showed
Asha showed the booking flow from an earlier project: pick a therapist, pick a time, fill a form.
Vikram showed the admin page of the same project.
After the meeting, Sara showed the team the front desk, the treatment rooms and the exercise room.
Asha will set up a shared folder for the project files and send Sara the link.
Meera said the team is glad to stop moving sessions by hand in the spreadsheet.
Sara asked to keep the same order of steps for the centre.

Opening hours and sessions
The centre is open Monday to Saturday.
On weekdays we open from 7 am to 7 pm.
On Saturday we open from 8 am to 12 noon.
There is no lunch break; therapists take turns.
Each therapy session lasts 45 minutes.
One session is for one patient with one therapist.

Pages we agreed on
1. A home page with the centre name, address, opening hours and phone number.
2. A list of the therapists, with a filter by specialty.
3. A calendar of free session times for the chosen therapist.
4. A booking form for the chosen time.
5. A page where a patient enters a booking code to see the booking or cancel it.
6. An admin page for the front desk: therapists, weekly hours and the list of bookings.
The centre is at 4 Palm Grove Lane, Eastbridge, and the phone number is 0491 570 157.

Booking form
The booking form asks only for name and phone.
Each booking gets a booking code of 6 characters, made of letters and digits.

Cancellation
A session can be cancelled up to 12 hours before it starts.
Handle cancellations properly.
A patient cancels with the booking code on the lookup page.

Admin page
Admin should see bookings quickly.
The front desk adds and edits therapists and sets their weekly hours.
Reports for the owner would be good to have.

Tools at the centre today
The front desk has two computers and a shared spreadsheet for the schedule.
The spreadsheet stays in use until the website is live.
Sara will export the current bookings so the team can see the real data.

Not part of this release
Patient accounts. A patient finds a booking again with the booking code.
