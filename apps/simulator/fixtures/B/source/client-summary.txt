Palm Grove Physio Centre: summary of the kickoff meeting
From: Sara, front desk lead. To: Asha Nair. Thursday 9 October.

Dear Asha,
Thank you for coming to the centre on Tuesday. Here is our summary of what we need.
Please tell me if anything here does not match your notes.

About the centre
Palm Grove Physio Centre treats sports injuries, back and neck pain, recovery after surgery
and the needs of older patients. This summary follows the order of the topics in the meeting.
We have four treatment rooms and one exercise room.

Our patients
Patients usually come for a course of six to ten sessions.
They often ask for the same therapist for every session, so the therapist list matters to them.

The home page
The home page should show our name, address, opening hours and phone number,
like the board at our door.

Our therapists
Meera Das, Sports injury.
Joseph Mathew, Back and neck.
Lina George, Post-surgery rehab.
Arjun Pillai, Elderly care.
Patients should get help choosing a therapist.

Booking rules
Sessions are one hour long.
Bookings open 21 days in advance.
Patients must state the injury area when they book.
Patients may book as many sessions as they need.
Patients can cancel any time before the session.

The booking pages
The booking page must work well on phones.
Each therapist has their own calendar of free times.
Patients use their booking code to look up a booking or to cancel it.

The admin page
The front desk uses the admin page to keep the list of therapists and their weekly hours.
Two people work at the front desk: me in the morning and Rekha in the afternoon.
Both of us will use the admin page, so please show us how it works.

Before we go live
Rekha and I would like to try the booking pages ourselves for one week,
with test bookings that we delete afterwards.
We will tell our patients about the website at the counter and in the treatment rooms.
Meera will put the link on the cards we give to new patients.

Thank you again. We look forward to the first version next Tuesday.
Sara
